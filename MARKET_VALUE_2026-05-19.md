# Market Value Snapshot 2026-05-19

## Current state

This is not a finished enterprise platform yet. Right now it is closer to a strong custom MVP:

- Telegram bot flow for customer request intake
- carrier offer, deposit and dispatch flow
- mobile workspace for customer and carrier roles
- admin dashboard with operations view, deposit desk and carrier heatmap
- 24/7 mail-monitoring tooling and outreach helpers

## Fair current valuation

For the product in its current state, a realistic market value is:

- `90k-180k RUB` as a codebase and internal MVP asset
- `180k-320k RUB` if handed over as a pilot-ready package with launch help, fixes and local deployment

That is the honest range before saying "hundreds of thousands" with confidence.

## Why the value is not higher yet

The main gaps against the full business brief are still:

- no live GPS or telematics control
- no real payment-provider integration, only deposit workflow and manual verification
- no full legal-entity and responsible-person document flow
- no ratings, dispute and quality loop yet
- no website integration and public web funnel yet
- no Google Sheets or CRM sync layer yet

## Value after the full brief is closed

If the remaining brief is completed cleanly and polished end-to-end, the range changes materially:

- `450k-900k RUB` for a serious custom pilot or white-label launch
- above that only after live payments, telematics, docs, org structure and operational analytics are fully production-grade

## Positioning note

Right now the honest sales framing is:

"We already have a working protected MVP with bot, app, admin and deposit-control logic. The current version is pilot-ready, and the full enterprise price is justified only after the remaining transport-control and document-flow block is fully finished."
